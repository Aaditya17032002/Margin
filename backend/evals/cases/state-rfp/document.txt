CITY OF NEW YORK
DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Request for Proposals — ARTS 311 CRM Systems Integrator
PIN: 84126RFP0031
Release Date: March 3, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
The Department seeks a qualified Systems Integrator to modernize its 311 CRM platform.
This procurement is conducted under the Procurement Policy Board Rules.
2. KEY DATESPre-proposal conference: March 24, 2026 at 10:00 AM.
Written questions are due 2026-04-02.
Proposals must be received no later than 2026-05-15 at 2:00 PM.
3. MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS
Proposers shall have completed a minimum of three (3) similar engagements in the past five years.
The Proposer must maintain an active registration in PASSPort prior to award.
Proposers are required to hold all licenses required by New York State law.
4. PROPOSAL FORMAT
Proposals shall not exceed 60 pages excluding required forms and resumes.
Text shall be Arial 11-point, double-spaced, with 1-inch margins.Proposals shall be submitted in three parts: Technical Proposal, Price Proposal, and Required Forms.
Each part shall be uploaded to PASSPort as a separate PDF not exceeding 25 MB in size.
5. REQUIRED FORMS
Proposers must submit Attachment A — Acknowledgment of Addenda.
Proposers must submit Attachment B — Doing Business Data Form.
Proposers must complete the MWBE Utilization Plan as described in Section 7.
6. EVALUATION
Proposals will be evaluated on Technical Approach (50%), Experience (30%), and Price (20%).
Technical Approach is more important than Price.
The City reserves the right to make an award on the basis of initial proposals.7. MWBE PARTICIPATION
The MWBE participation goal for this contract is 30 percent.
Proposers shall certify their MWBE status in accordance with Local Law 1 of 2013.
8. SECURITY
The Contractor may not export, process, access or store City Data outside the United States.
The Contractor is responsible for adhering to the City's IT security policies.
